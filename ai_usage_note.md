## A specific example of AI output being checked and corrected

During test-writing, a bug was found in the confidence-audit logic: the phrase "could **not be confirmed**" was being counted as *definitive* language (because it contains the word "confirmed") when it should have counted as *hedging* language (because "not confirmed" is actually an expression of uncertainty). This produced an incorrect verdict on a synthetic test case.

This was caught by an automated test (`test_low_score_with_hedging_is_consistent_not_flagged` initially failing unexpectedly), not by manual inspection — which is itself a small demonstration of the same principle this whole product is built around: don't trust a system's output just because it looks confident; verify it. The fix (a span-masking approach so overlapping phrase matches aren't double-counted, see `_find_phrases` in `confidence_audit.py`) is documented in the code and in the README's Limitations section.

## What was *not* AI-generated

- The two customer interviews (questions asked, answers received) are real interviews conducted by the team with real interviewees (Aditya and Daksh). Claude was not present for and did not generate any interview content — it was given the team's real notes afterward and asked to format them into the required deliverable structure.
- All quantitative findings in the Data Validation Report were produced by running actual analysis code against the actual provided dataset, not inferred or estimated by AI from general knowledge.
- Product decisions (which direction to build, what to prioritize, what to leave out) were made by the team, informed by AI-assisted analysis and drafting but not delegated to AI.

## Constraints followed

- No paid AI tools, APIs, or credits were used, consistent with the task's "no paid tools" rule.
- No AI-generated claims about the dataset were accepted without being run and checked against the actual files — every number in every deliverable is reproducible by re-running the corresponding script in this repository.
